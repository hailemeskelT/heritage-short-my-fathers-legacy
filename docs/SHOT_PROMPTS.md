All scenes are AI-generated illustrative reconstructions, not archival images or proof of specific events. Use a generic, non-identifiable father character unless written informed consent is recorded for an identifiable likeness. Do not invent exact village details, crops, dates, disputes, quotations, ceremonial clothing, or historical incidents.
