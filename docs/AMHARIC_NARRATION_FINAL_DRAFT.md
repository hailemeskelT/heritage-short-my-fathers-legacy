# Amharic Narration — Story-Based Draft for Review

**Title:** የአባቴ ውርስ — My Father's Legacy  
**Setting identified by the storyteller:** North Shewa, Ethiopia  
**Target film duration:** 68 seconds  
**Narration language:** Amharic  

> This script is based only on the family account supplied by the filmmaker. It does not add a date, village, crop, specific dispute, quotation, or named historical event. Have a fluent Amharic speaker review naturalness and pronunciation. Measure the generated audio; the storyboard durations and subtitle timecodes are provisional until checked against the final narration.

## Shot 01

**Amharic:** አንዳንድ ታሪኮች በመጻሕፍት ውስጥ አይጻፉም።

**English meaning:** Some stories are never written in books.

**Visual purpose:** Establish the North Shewa setting identified in the family account without inventing a precise location.

## Shot 02

**Amharic:** በቤታችን ውስጥ ይኖራሉ፤ ከትውልድ ወደ ትውልድም ይተላለፋሉ።

**English meaning:** They live in our homes and pass from one generation to the next.

**Visual purpose:** Introduce farming as an important part of the father's life.

## Shot 03

**Amharic:** የቤተሰባችን ታሪክ ከታሪካዊቷና ውብ የሰሜን ሸዋ ምድር ጋር የተያያዘ ነው።

**English meaning:** Our family story is rooted in the historic, beautiful land of North Shewa.

**Visual purpose:** Introduce the father as the central subject while avoiding an unconsented likeness.

## Shot 04

**Amharic:** ዛሬ የማካፍላችሁ ታሪክ የአባቴ ታሪክ ነው።

**English meaning:** Today, I share my father's story.

**Visual purpose:** Connect the father's identity with farming and diligence.

## Shot 05

**Amharic:** በግብርና ሥራው ታታሪነትን በተግባር ያሳየ ሲሆን፣ ለአካባቢው ሰዎችም አርአያ ነበር።

**English meaning:** Through farming, he demonstrated hard work and became a role model to people around him.

**Visual purpose:** Represent the father's effort to raise the family with love and good values.

## Shot 06

**Amharic:** ቤተሰባችንን በፍቅርና በሥነ ምግባር ለማሳደግ ሌት ተቀን ደክሟል።

**English meaning:** He worked day and night to raise our family with love and good values.

**Visual purpose:** Visualize family affection and moral guidance without inventing a particular event.

## Shot 07

**Amharic:** በግብርናም ሆነ በማኅበራዊ ኑሮው ለአካባቢው ሰዎች ትልቅ አርአያ ነበር።

**English meaning:** In both farming and community life, he was an example to those around him.

**Visual purpose:** Show the father's respected place in community and social life.

## Shot 08

**Amharic:** በአካባቢው ባህል መሠረት በሽምግልናና በእርቅ ሥራ በመሳተፍ ማኅበረሰቡን ያገለግል ነበር።

**Visual purpose:** Represent the father's role in shimgilna (traditional mediation) and reconciliation, as described by the family.

## Shot 09

**Amharic:** ያን ጊዜ ቀላል አልነበረም። ግን ፈተናን በትዕግሥት መቋቋምንና በታታሪነት መቀጠልን አስተማረን።

**English meaning:** Those times were not easy. He taught us to face hardship with patience and perseverance.

**Visual purpose:** Express the lesson of facing difficulties patiently and continuing to work diligently.

## Shot 10

**Amharic:** ዓመታት ያልፋሉ፣ ትውልዶችም ይቀያየራሉ። ከአባቴ የተማርነው ፍቅር፣ ትጋትና መከባበር ከእኛ ጋር ይኖራል። ታሪካችን ማንነታችን ነው።

**English meaning:** Years pass and generations change. The love, diligence and respect we learned from my father live on. Our story is our identity.

**Visual purpose:** Conclude with the idea that the father's values continue across generations and include the required AI disclosure.

## Voice direction

Use a warm, sincere, reflective Amharic narrator. The narrator is not imitating the father's voice. Speak naturally and clearly, with pauses between thoughts. Do not clone or imitate a real person's voice without recorded informed consent.
