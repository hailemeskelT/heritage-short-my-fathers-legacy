# Subtitle Timing Note

The SRT timecodes are provisional and follow the current 68-second storyboard shot boundaries. After generating the actual Amharic narration, listen to it, measure its duration, and adjust each subtitle cue to match the spoken words. Keep captions to no more than two lines, use readable line lengths, and keep them in the vertical-video safe area. Do not treat the present timecodes as verified audio synchronization.
